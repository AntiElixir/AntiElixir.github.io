Personal notes on basketball techniques, focusing on areas I'm working to improve.

## Mindset

1. **Remember the triple threat.** Always be ready to shoot, pass, or drive.
2. **My biggest issue:** Losing the ball under pressure, mainly due to high center of gravity. This affects both offense and defense.
3. **Communicate.** Don't be silent on the court — talk to teammates.

## Warm-up

- **Hip mobility is crucial.** After sitting for long periods, opening up the hips feels amazing and prevents injury.

## The First Step

1. **Drive with the hips.** The first step should land on the ball of the foot, not the whole foot. Push off with the outside edge to reduce Achilles pressure.
2. **Big steps.** Larger steps naturally lead to ball-of-foot landing. Small steps cause forward lean and loss of control.
3. **Technique cues:**
   - Staggered stance with feet wide
   - Pocket dribbles: pull the ball back before driving forward
   - Trigger step with heel up (tap the ground before exploding)
   - Lower the inside shoulder

## During the Drive

1. **Rhythm:** Quick crossover, slow between the legs.
2. **After crossover:** Float the ball, both feet move forward together, then transition to crossover or between-the-legs.
3. **Crossover mechanics:**
   - Before: in-and-out dribble, ball away from body
   - Slightly lift left foot, raise right hand, push off back foot
   - Key: drop center of gravity to change direction, not just split the legs
   - Open the hips! Push off the inside of the back foot for better balance
   - When crossing left, move right hand to the left side for protection/fake
   - Explode immediately after left foot touches — speed comes from this moment
   - Ball should bounce near the right foot with large amplitude
   - After crossover, right foot moves to roughly level with left foot, toe pointing out
   - Once right hand touches the ball, push it forward — don't keep it in the pocket!

## Defensive Stance

- Keep low to absorb contact (practice: touch defender's knee)
- Seek contact early
- If defender is stronger or aggressive: stop, spin, and drive again or change direction

## Key Principles

- **Want to go forward? First go backward.** Push off before exploding.
- **Never have the ball in front.** Always keep it back.
- **Auxiliary arm up and forward.**
- **Early gather:** Over the shoulder or under the knees.
- **Elbow in:** Keep arm, elbow, and shoulder close to defender.
- **Turn hips forward** to drive toward the lane.
- **Toes should point toward the basket!**
