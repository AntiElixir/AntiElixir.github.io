A summer research project independently analyzing the statistical characteristics of mesoscale eddies in the North Indian Ocean and their ecological impacts.

## Data Processing

Processed multi-source remote sensing and reanalysis datasets using Python:
- AVISO sea surface height
- META eddy trajectories
- GLORYS temperature/salinity data
- OCCCI chlorophyll-a data

## Methodology

Constructed a North Indian Ocean mesoscale eddy database with feature extraction (radius, lifecycle, temperature, salinity, chlorophyll anomalies) and spatiotemporal matching. Applied statistical analysis and Composite Analysis to extract physical and biological properties associated with eddies.

## Key Findings

- Cyclonic and anticyclonic eddies show distinct signatures in temperature, salinity, and chlorophyll anomalies
- Eddy-induced upwelling/downwelling significantly affects local primary productivity
- Seasonal variation in eddy characteristics correlates with monsoon dynamics
