This project explores the intersection of magnetics tactile sensors and robotics learning. The goal is to understand how rich tactile feedback can improve robot manipulation and embodied intelligence.

## Background

Traditional robotic manipulation relies heavily on visual feedback, but many tasks require fine-grained touch information. Magnetics tactile sensors offer high-resolution, low-latency force and contact data that can complement vision.

## Research Questions

- How can magnetics tactile data be integrated into robot learning pipelines?
- What representations best capture tactile information for policy learning?
- Can tactile feedback improve sample efficiency in manipulation tasks?

## Current Progress

Under the guidance of faculty at Puyuan College, I am setting up the experimental pipeline and exploring sensor integration with existing robot learning frameworks.
