Participated in the 2026 National University Student AI Finance Research & Training Camp, building a quantitative research framework for commodity futures.

## Framework

Built a quantitative research framework covering **30 commodity futures varieties** and **57 conceptual factors** for the Chinese market. Used fixed real-contract mapping, 5-day holding periods, and 5bps one-way cost conventions to ensure backtest tradability.

## Machine Learning Track

Led the tree-model and ensemble-learning track:
- Independently built and optimized regularized **LightGBM, XGBoost, and Random Forest** models
- Conducted horizontal evaluation against traditional linear baselines (Ridge, equal-weight combinations) and deep learning models
- Implemented **2019-2025 expanding-window out-of-sample testing**
- Verified net-return stability under cost erosion and year-drift conditions

## Results

**Won 1st Prize in Theme 7 comprehensive evaluation** (top 10 out of ~200 participants).

Contributed to writing the full research protocol and reproducibility documentation. Code and data interfaces organized in the team GitHub repository.
