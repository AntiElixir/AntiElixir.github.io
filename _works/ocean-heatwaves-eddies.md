Investigating how mesoscale eddies regulate the formation and development of ocean heatwaves, and assessing the impact of subsurface heatwaves on primary productivity.

## Approach

The full pipeline is implemented in Python, involving cleaning, aligning, and modeling multi-source observational data. We built a database of ocean heatwaves and mesoscale eddies, and completed statistical modeling of their spatiotemporal coupling relationships.

## Key Questions

- How do different types of mesoscale eddies (cyclonic vs. anticyclonic) modulate heatwave intensity and duration?
- What is the impact of subsurface heatwaves on chlorophyll-a concentration and primary productivity?
- Can we predict heatwave- eddy interactions using statistical models?

## Data Sources

- Satellite sea surface temperature (SST)
- Eddy trajectory datasets
- Reanalysis temperature and salinity profiles
- Ocean color (chlorophyll-a) data
